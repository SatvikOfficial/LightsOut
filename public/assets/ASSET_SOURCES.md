## Scope notes

- The HD knight is the only free character in its pack; the other eight
  characters are in the paid edition and were not downloaded. The free knight
  sheet is used for Viktor.
- The Heroes and Dread Realms downloads are free demos, not their paid full
  packs. Their eight-direction poses are static; no authored movement or combat
  animation is implied.
- The Blade Assassin is used for Vessa, not a canon boss. Dracula uses the
  vampire sheet, Vargulf the bone-crusher pose, and the Lamplighter the
  doll-like undead pose. Those boss images are visual matches, not dedicated
  illustrations.
- Miregrave art supplies hamlet buildings and region-appropriate trees/reeds
  for the Forest, Mire, Cinder, and Hamlet. Hamlet buildings use the shared
  deterministic decoration and collision data.
- The vampire sheet has four directions and three poses per direction; it is
  not a full combat animation set.
- The Miregrave product includes generative-AI-created graphics. Its original
  disclosure and license are kept with the downloaded pack.
- No dedicated free Frostfall snow/ice environment or Cinder lava pack is
  included.
- The 32x32 OpenGameArt pack is supplemental art, not the high-detail primary
  character style, so it is retained locally but not mixed into the current
  higher-resolution character/environment presentation.
- The Cursed Woodland free preview was downloaded for evaluation but excluded
  from this public asset library: its page grants commercial use while its
  included README says the release license is not finalized. Do not ship it
  unless that conflict is resolved with the creator.
