CONTENTS

- 12 high-resolution medieval buildings

- 38 transparent PNG assets in total

No ground textures are included in this edition.

TECHNICAL NOTES

- Native high-resolution PNG files; no resampling was applied to the product assets.
- All assets use a true transparent background with binary alpha (0 or 255).
- RGB values beneath fully transparent pixels are black to prevent white halos.
- Buildings use a coherent top-down 3/4 perspective with south-facing entrances.
- Road images are pre-built pieces, not a grid-based seamless terrain tileset.
